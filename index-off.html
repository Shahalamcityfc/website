About The Club

Our City. Our Colours. Our Club.

Founded in 2018 and registered under the Malaysia Sports Commissioner (C-SGR-00022), Shah Alam City FC was built to bring competitive football to the heart of Selangor. Known as The Wolves, we play for the city and fight for the badge.
